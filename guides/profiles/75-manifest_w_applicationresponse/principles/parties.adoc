[[parties-and-roles]]
= Parties and roles

The table below gives the definitions of the parties and roles involved in the Manifest process.

[cols="2,8",options="header",]
|====
|Business partners |Definition
|*Logistics Operator* a|

The Logistics Operator is the legal person or organization who operates, or arranges the operation of, the means of transport (vessel, aircraft, truck or wagon) that the Manifest describes.

|*Authority* a|

The Authority is a public body (e.g. customs, port state control, terminal control) that requires a Manifest before, during or after a journey, in order to control cargo, crew and/or passengers.

|*Consignor* a|

The consignor is the legal person or organization who is sending the goods described in the Manifest's shipment.

|*Consignee* a|

The consignee is the legal person or organization to whom the goods described in the Manifest's shipment are addressed.

|====

[cols="2,9",options="header",]
|====
|Role/actor |Definition
|*Sending Logistics Operator (cac:SendingLogisticsOperatorParty)* a|

The party who issues the Manifest. This is normally the Logistics Operator responsible for the means of transport.

|*Authority (cac:AuthorityParty)* a|

The Authority or regulator who receives the Manifest, when the Manifest is sent to a public body.

|*Consignor (cac:ConsignorParty)* a|

Consignor is the party responsible for sending the goods described in the shipment.

|*Consignee (cac:ConsigneeParty)* a|

Consignee is the party receiving the goods described in the shipment.

|*Sender (cac:SenderParty)* a|

Used in the Application Response. The party sending the Application Response - normally the same party as the Authority (or other receiving party) on the Manifest.

|*Receiver (cac:ReceiverParty)* a|

Used in the Application Response. The party receiving the Application Response - normally the same party as the Sending Logistics Operator on the Manifest.

|====

Note that the Manifest can be exchanged directly between two commercial parties (e.g. a carrier and a terminal operator) as well as between a Logistics Operator and a public Authority. In both cases the responding party plays the *Sender* role on the Application Response, and the party that issued the Manifest plays the *Receiver* role.
