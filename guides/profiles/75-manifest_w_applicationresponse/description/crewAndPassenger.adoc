[[crew-and-passenger]]
= Crew and passenger

A Manifest can list the crew (`cac:CrewPerson`) operating the means of transport, and - where relevant, such as for ferry or air manifests - the passengers (`cac:PassengerPerson`) being carried. Both are repeatable, and both are optional: a cargo-only manifest (e.g. a road freight manifest) will typically include crew but no passengers.
